# Changelog

What changed in each release, in plain language. The latest entry is also the "what's new" text for the
Web Store submission.

## 1.3.0

- **Keep your subject when you reuse an email.** The EasyMerge button in the compose toolbar now has an
  "Include subject" checkbox. Uncheck it to replace only the message and keep the subject you already
  typed. EasyMerge remembers your choice.
- **Undo a replace.** Replaced a draft by mistake? Press Cmd+Z (Ctrl+Z on Windows) to get your old subject
  and message back. You can also click "Undo replace" in the button's popup. Undo works until you start
  typing in the draft.

## 1.2.0

- **Attachments go to everyone.** When you send to several people, each person's email now includes the
  files attached to your draft.

## 1.1.0

- **One email per recipient.** Put everyone in the To field and EasyMerge shows a row for each person,
  fills in what it can (first name, company), and sends each of them their own email.

## 1.0.0

- First release: type wildcards like _FIRST_NAME in a Gmail draft and fill them in when you click Send.
  Your last EasyMerge email can be brought back from the compose toolbar to send again.
